Privacy notice

Your privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Wren is a product we are still developing. This website helps us learn how many people would want it, and lets you reserve a spot so we can tell you when it is available. This notice explains what we collect on this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you type it in to reserve a spot.
  • Your answers to the optional questions in the reserve form: your baby's stage, the feature you want most, and whether you would buy at the price shown. You can skip all of them.
  • Whether you asked for product updates (the checkbox is off unless you tick it).
  • Anonymous visit and click data: a random visitor id stored in your browser, which page you viewed, whether you scrolled to pricing, which Buy button you clicked, which plan and price you saw, the general type of device (phone, tablet or computer), the website that sent you here, and ad campaign tags in the link you followed (such as utm_source). The random id is not linked to your name, and we do not use it to follow you on other websites.

What we never collect

We do not collect any payment or card details. Nothing is sold on this website and you are never charged. We do not record audio, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies from other companies on this site.

Why we collect it

  • To measure how much interest there is in Wren, and which plans and prices people prefer.
  • To email you when pre-orders open, if you reserved a spot.
  • To send you occasional product updates, only if you ticked the box for them.

We will never sell your email address or share it with anyone for their own marketing. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe.

Where it is stored

Reservations and visit data are stored in a private Google Sheet that only our team can open. Your browser also keeps the random visitor id, the price you were shown, and your email (if you reserved) in its own local storage, so the site remembers you on your next visit. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

How long we keep it

We keep your email and answers until Wren launches and for up to 12 months after that, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. Anonymous visit and click data is kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.

Your choices and how to ask for deletion

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time. Email privacy@example.com from the address you reserved with, and write "Delete my data". We will confirm within 30 days.

Children

This website is for parents and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use your email, we will tell you by email first.
