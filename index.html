Quick answers

When does it ship?

We're still building it. Pre-order and you'll be first to hear when it's ready. Nothing is charged today.

Is it a medical device?

No. Dawn doesn't diagnose anything and doesn't replace your pediatrician or safe sleep: baby on their back, on a firm, flat surface, nothing else in the crib.

Is it safe for my baby?

Dawn is designed to be gentle. The band is soft organic cotton with no wires in the fabric, and the 18 g pod has a smooth, rounded silicone shell, so nothing hard presses on your baby's skin. It uses low-energy Bluetooth to talk to the dock.

Has it been tested?

Not yet. Dawn is still a prototype. Before it ships, it has to pass independent safety testing for baby products, and we'll share the results here. Until then, nothing is sold or charged.
