10:15 pm Why the chest

Measured where breathing happens.

A foot or a wrist can only guess at breathing. The chest moves with every breath, so that is where Wren sits.

InhaleExhale

Breathing, read directly

Each breath lifts the chest and gently stretches the band. The pod's motion sensor follows that rise and fall, so breathing rate is measured breath by breath, not inferred from somewhere else.

Stays on through the night

A soft stretch band with no straps to wriggle out of. The pod sits flat in a padded pocket, so it stays in place through kicks, wriggles and nighttime feeds.

Senses sleep position

Sitting on the torso, Wren can tell back from side from tummy, and sends a rollover alert to your phone when your baby turns onto their tummy.

12:30 am What it tracks

Ten signals. One calm screen.

  • Breathing rate

    breaths/min

    Read from the rise and fall of the chest, breath by breath.

  • Heart rate

    bpm

    An optical sensor on the skin side of the pod, sampled all night.

  • Blood oxygen

    SpO2 %

    Oxygen saturation and how it trends through the night.

  • Skin temperature

    °C / °F

    Chest skin temperature, so you can see if they are running warm or cool.

  • Sleep position

    back · side · tummy

    Knows which way they are lying, with a rollover alert for tummy sleeping.

  • Active vs quiet sleep

    sleep state · h:mm

    How the night splits between active sleep, quiet sleep and awake.

  • Movement & wake windows

    motion · min awake

    Wriggles, wake-ups and how long it has been since the last sleep.

  • Cry & cough detection

    events/night

    Processed on the pod itself. Audio is never recorded to the cloud or uploaded.

  • Room temperature & humidity

    °C / °F · % RH

    The charging dock keeps an eye on the nursery climate.

  • Feeding & diaper log

    time · mL / oz

    Log feeds and changes in a tap, lined up with the night's sleep.

5:50 am How it works

Three steps to a calmer night.

  1. 1

    Slip on the band

    Click the pod into its pocket and wrap the band around your baby's chest, over a bodysuit or on skin. It takes about ten seconds.

  2. 2

    Pair with the app

    Plug in the dock, open the Wren app and follow three screens. The pod talks to the dock, and the dock talks to your phone.

  3. 3

    Rest, and glance when you like

    Check live readings any time, get a notification when a reading moves outside the ranges you set, and wake up to the night's summary.

7:05 am Questions

Good morning. Ask away.

Is Wren a medical device?

No. Wren is a concept product in development and is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and it does not replace safe sleep practices or advice from your pediatrician.

Always follow safe sleep guidance: baby on their back, on a firm, flat surface, with nothing else in the crib.

When does it ship?

We are finishing development now and orders are not open yet. Reserve a spot and you will be the first to hear when pre-orders open, including the final price and shipping date. Reserving is free and does not commit you to anything.

Is my data private?

Privacy is built into the design. Cry and cough detection runs on the pod itself, and audio is never uploaded. Readings are encrypted on their way to your phone and in storage, and your family's data will never be sold.

On this website, we only collect what is described in our privacy notice.

Which size do I need?

There are three sizes: Small for 0-3 months (chest 32-40 cm), Medium for 3-9 months (38-47 cm) and Large for 9-18 months (44-54 cm). The band stretches, so each size covers a range. If you are between sizes, measure around the chest just under the armpits and pick the size where that number falls in the middle.

What is it made of?

The band is a soft organic cotton knit with a little stretch, with flat seams and no wires in the fabric. The pod has a smooth, rounded silicone shell and sits in a padded pocket, so nothing hard presses on your baby's skin.

How do I wash it?

Pop the pod out, then machine wash the band cold on a gentle cycle and lay it flat to dry. Wipe the pod with a damp cloth. Many parents keep a second band so one is always fresh.
