Why the chest

We got this wrong first.

Our first prototype was a band for the wrist and ankle. We moved it to the chest, because that is where breathing actually happens.

InhaleExhale

Breathing, measured where it happens

Each breath lifts the chest and gently stretches the band. The pod's motion sensor follows that rise and fall, so breathing rate is read breath by breath, not guessed from a foot or a wrist.

Stays on through the night

A soft stretch band with a wide hook-and-loop tab and nothing to wriggle out of. The pod sits flat against the chest, so it stays in place through kicks, wriggles and night feeds.

Senses sleep position

Sitting on the torso, Dawn can tell back from side from tummy, and sends a rollover alert to your phone when your baby turns onto their tummy.

What it tracks

Everything you check at 3 a.m., in one place.

  • Breathing rate

    breaths/min

    Read from the rise and fall of the chest, breath by breath.

  • Heart rate

    bpm

    An optical sensor on the skin side of the pod, sampled all night.

  • Blood oxygen

    SpO2 %

    Oxygen saturation and how it trends through the night.

  • Active vs quiet sleep

    sleep state · h:mm

    How the night splits between active sleep, quiet sleep and awake.

  • Skin temperature

    °C / °F

    Running warm after a vaccine? See the trend, not a single guess.

  • Sleep position

    back · side · tummy

    Knows which way they are lying, with a rollover alert for tummy sleeping.

  • Movement & wake windows

    motion · min awake

    Wriggles, wake-ups and how long it has been since the last sleep.

  • Cry & cough detection

    events/night

    Processed on the pod itself. Audio is never recorded to the cloud or uploaded.

  • Room temperature & humidity

    °C / °F · % RH

    The charging dock keeps an eye on the nursery climate.

  • Feeding & diaper log

    time · mL / oz

    Feeds, wet and dirty diapers in a tap, lined up with the night's sleep.

How it works

Three steps to a calmer night.

  1. 1

    Slip on the band

    Click the pod into the band and wrap it around your baby's chest, over a bodysuit or on skin. It takes about ten seconds.

  2. 2

    Pair with the app

    Plug in the dock, open the Dawn app and follow three screens. The pod talks to the dock, and the dock talks to your phone.

  3. 3

    Rest, and glance when you like

    Check live vitals any time, get an alert when something is off from your baby's normal, and wake up to one morning score.

Questions

Good morning. Ask away.

Is Dawn a medical device?

No. Dawn is a concept product in development and is not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and it does not replace safe sleep practices or advice from your pediatrician.

Always follow safe sleep guidance: baby on their back, on a firm, flat surface, with nothing else in the crib.

When does it ship?

We are finishing development now and orders are not open yet. Reserve a spot and you will be the first to hear when pre-orders open, including the final price and shipping date. Reserving is free and does not commit you to anything.

Membership or one-time: what's the difference?

Both come with the band, pod and dock, live vitals, alerts and the green, yellow, red light. The membership adds the morning score and trends, Mira, doctor-ready reports, unlimited history, the parent community and the vaccine tracker, billed once a year. The one-time plan is a single payment with 7 days of history.

What is Mira?

Mira is Dawn's AI guide. It looks at your baby's readings against their own normal and suggests calm next steps, like what to try when a baby won't settle, and it tells you clearly when it's time to call your pediatrician. Mira gives general guidance, not medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Is my data private?

Your data is yours. Cry and cough detection runs on the pod itself, and audio is never uploaded. Readings are encrypted on their way to your phone and in storage, you choose what to share with your pediatrician, and your family's data will never be sold.

On this website, we only collect what is described in our privacy notice.

Which size do I need?

There are three sizes: Small for 0-3 months (chest 32-40 cm), Medium for 3-9 months (38-47 cm) and Large for 9-18 months (44-54 cm). The band stretches, so each size covers a range. If you are between sizes, measure around the chest just under the armpits and pick the size where that number falls in the middle.

What is it made of?

The band is a soft organic cotton knit with a little stretch, with flat seams and no wires in the fabric. The 18-gram pod has a smooth, rounded silicone shell, so nothing hard or heavy presses on your baby's skin.

How do I wash it?

Pop the pod out, then machine wash the band cold on a gentle cycle and lay it flat to dry. Wipe the pod with a damp cloth. Many parents keep a second band so one is always fresh.

What's next for Dawn?

The band comes first. After that we're working on a movement monitor, a smart cradle and a smart diaper that all share one app, growing with your baby from birth to toddler.
